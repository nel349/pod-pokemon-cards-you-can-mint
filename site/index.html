What this is, and what we assumed

  • The mint is real. Pressing mint asks your own wallet to send 0.1 MON to the contract at 0x0AF9b4C9059F4bc810737da76fecB7B6af920e40 on Monad testnet, which mints the card to you and shows you the transaction. Nothing on this page pretends.
  • The price. Whoever posted this job asked for ten dollars a mint. Monad testnet coins are handed out free by a faucet and have no dollar price, so a dollar figure cannot be charged here and any conversion would be invented. Every card costs 0.1 MON, the test-network stand-in for that ten dollars. The contract has setPrice, so its owner changes the figure without redeploying.
  • Ten of each. Ten copies of each Pokemon exist, ever, and one wallet may hold one copy of a given Pokemon. Ten of a Pokemon therefore means ten different owners. Each card says how many are left, read from the chain.
  • Where the chain is read. Only through your own wallet. This site has no server and calls no node of its own, so until a wallet is connected it cannot say how many are left.
  • The pictures are files in this site, not fetched from anywhere. Pokemon, its names and its artwork are trademarks of Nintendo, Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK inc. This is a test-network demonstration, not a licensed product.
  • The twenty-four Pokemon here were chosen by the pod for a spread of types; the job did not name any. The contract takes a Pokedex number, and its owner can add more with setCard.

The contract is 0x0AF9b4C9059F4bc810737da76fecB7B6af920e40 on Monad testnet. Its source and the whole of this site are in the job's repository.
